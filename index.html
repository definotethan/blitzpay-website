The way it works now

Payroll day is a spreadsheet and a long evening

Most growing SMEs run payroll by exporting the time logs, pasting them into a sheet, and fixing the rest by hand. It works — until it quietly doesn't.

  • Every cutoff starts from zero

    Copy the export, paste it in, re-check the formulas, chase the shifts that look wrong. Same work, twice a month, forever.

  • Holidays are checked by hand

    Someone has to remember which dates were regular holidays, who worked them, and who is owed premium pay for it.

  • A blank cell costs real money

    One row left unfinished and someone is short-paid. Nothing in a spreadsheet tells you it happened.

How BlitzPay works

Three steps, and you keep the final say

No new hardware and no new app for your staff. They clock in exactly as they do today.

  1. Send the export

    Drop in the .csv or .xlsx your time-clock app already produces. No re-typing, no new system for your team to learn.

  2. We compute the cutoff

    Hours, overtime, night differential, holidays, government deductions and service charge — using your pay rules, not a generic template.

  3. Review, then release

    Anything unusual — a missing clock-out, an unusually long shift — is flagged for you to approve or correct. Nothing is paid without your sign-off.

What you get each cutoff

One run, two outputs

For your staff

A payslip each

A clear breakdown of earnings, deductions and take-home pay — the kind of payslip an employee can actually read without asking you to explain it.

For you

A review sheet

Every employee's totals in one place, with flagged time logs and the reason each one needs a look. Check it, approve it, then pay.

The way it works now

Payroll day is a spreadsheet and a long evening

Most growing SMEs run payroll by exporting the time logs, pasting them into a sheet, and fixing the rest by hand. It works — until it quietly doesn't.

  • Every cutoff starts from zero

    Copy the export, paste it in, re-check the formulas, chase the shifts that look wrong. Same work, twice a month, forever.

  • Holidays are checked by hand

    Someone has to remember which dates were regular holidays, who worked them, and who is owed premium pay for it.

  • A blank cell costs real money

    One row left unfinished and someone is short-paid. Nothing in a spreadsheet tells you it happened.

How BlitzPay works

Three steps, and you keep the final say

No new hardware and no new app for your staff. They clock in exactly as they do today.

  1. Send the export

    Drop in the .csv or .xlsx your time-clock app already produces. No re-typing, no new system for your team to learn.

  2. We compute the cutoff

    Hours, overtime, night differential, holidays, government deductions and service charge — using your pay rules, not a generic template.

  3. Review, then release

    Anything unusual — a missing clock-out, an unusually long shift — is flagged for you to approve or correct. Nothing is paid without your sign-off.

Built for Philippine payroll

The rules that make local payroll fiddly, handled

Each one is a setting, not a hard-coded assumption. Your rates and your practice decide the figures, and any rule that changes pay is switched on deliberately.

  • Holiday pay Regular and special days

    Government rates for regular holidays and special non-working days, including work on the day itself, applied per date in the cutoff.

  • Labor Code Art. 86 Night differential

    An extra 10% for every hour worked between 10 PM and 6 AM, counted from the clock-ins themselves.

  • Service charge Shared by days worked

    The pool from your sales report, split across eligible staff by the days each of them actually worked, per branch.

  • Contributions SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG

    Deducted on the cutoff you choose, for the staff they apply to, with new hires held until their paperwork is complete.

  • Overtime Premiums on your terms

    Overtime beyond the standard day, at the rate your business already uses, not a generic template.

  • Allowances and advances The rest of the payslip

    Daily allowances, incentives and cash advances, entered once per cutoff and carried into every payslip.

Questions we get

Before you ask

Do my staff need to learn a new app?

No. They clock in and out exactly as they do today. BlitzPay reads the export your existing time clock or POS already produces.

What happens when the time logs have mistakes?

They get flagged instead of quietly paid: a missing clock-out, a shift that runs past midnight, a person in the logs who isn't on your staff list. You correct them with a reason, and the original export is never edited.

Can I see what changed since the last run?

Yes. Every figure that moves is listed against the previous run, and every correction records who made it, when, and why. Payroll you can explain to an employee, or an auditor.
